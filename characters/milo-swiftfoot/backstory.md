# Backstory: Milo Swiftfoot

Milo Swiftfoot was born in the creaking back of a covered wagon, the son of a halfling circus family that wandered the trade roads of the Dalelands. By the age of ten he could juggle, tumble, and pick a pocket faster than a blink — but it was the singing that set him apart. His voice could soothe a crying babe, silence a brawling tavern, and, on one memorable night, talk a hungry owlbear out of eating the family's prize pony.

When a passing elven minstrel heard Milo perform in a dusty crossroads inn, she pressed her own worn lute into his hands and vanished before dawn. Milo spent the next years learning every song the road could teach him, weaving spells into his music until he could make a dragon's hoard seem dull and a dragon's heart feel light.

Now a wandering performer with a gift for charming his way out of trouble, Milo uses his music to lift spirits, expose corruption, and lend courage to the downtrodden. He believes every heart has a song worth singing — even the blackened ones — and he'll happily sing it to you for a meal, a roof, or a good story to carry to the next town.
