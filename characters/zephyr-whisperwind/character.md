# Zephyr Whisperwind

## Basic Information
- **Race:** Scourge Aasimar
- **Class:** Sorcerer (Storm Sorcery) - Level 3
- **Background:** Hermit
- **Alignment:** Neutral
- **Deity:** Silvanus (God of Wild Nature) / Elemental Powers

## Ability Scores
- **Strength:** 8 (-1)
- **Dexterity:** 14 (+2)
- **Constitution:** 14 (+2)
- **Intelligence:** 10 (+0)
- **Wisdom:** 12 (+1)
- **Charisma:** 16 (+3)

## Combat Statistics
- **Armor Class (AC):** 12 (15 with *Mage Armor*)
- **Hit Points (HP):** 20
- **Speed:** 30 ft
- **Proficiency Bonus:** +2
- **Passive Perception:** 13

## Proficiencies & Languages
- **Armor:** None
- **Weapons:** Daggers, darts, slings, quarterstaffs, light crossbows
- **Tools:** Herbalism kit
- **Saving Throws:** Constitution, Charisma
- **Skills:** Arcana, Medicine, Nature, Religion
- **Languages:** Common, Celestial, Primordial (Auran)

## Features & Traits
- **Celestial Resistance:** Resistance to necrotic and radiant damage.
- **Healing Hands:** Touch a creature to restore HP equal to your level (3 HP).
- **Radiant Consumption (Aasimar Trait):** Emit searing light dealing radiant damage to surrounding creatures and extra radiant damage on attacks for 1 minute.
- **Wind Speaker:** Read, write, and speak Primordial; understand elemental spirits.
- **Tempestuous Magic:** Bonus action after casting a 1st+ level spell to fly up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Font of Magic & Metamagic:** 3 Sorcery Points; Quickened Spell, Twinned Spell.
- **Spellcasting:** Cantrips (*Shocking Grasp*, *Ray of Frost*, *Gust*, *Thunderclap*); Level 1-2 Spells (*Thunderwave*, *Witch Bolt*, *Shatter*, *Mage Armor*).

## Equipment
- Quarterstaff, Dagger
- Component Pouch, Herbalism Kit
- Winter Clothes, Journal of Weather Observations
- Dungeoneer's Pack, 10 gold pieces
