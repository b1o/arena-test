# Backstory: Zephyr Whisperwind

Zephyr was born during a violent superstorm that tore across the high peaks of the Spine of the World. Infused from birth with the divine radiance of his aasimar heritage and the raw, turbulent energy of the tempest, Zephyr's presence often manifested as static sparks and sudden gusts of wind whenever his emotions ran high.

Fearing his uncontrollable magic might bring harm to his village, Zephyr exiled himself to a remote mountain peak. In solitude, he meditated alongside eagles and elemental spirits, learning to harmonize his inner celestial spark with the gale-force storms of the mountain. A vision of an impending unnatural cataclysm eventually called him down from his solitude.

Now wandering the civilized realms, Zephyr seeks to maintain the balance of elemental forces, protect wild places from devastation, and learn to fully command the tempest raging within his soul.
