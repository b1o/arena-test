# Thorin Ironbreaker

## Basic Information
- **Race:** Mountain Dwarf
- **Class:** Cleric (Forge Domain) - Level 3
- **Background:** Guild Artisan (Blacksmith)
- **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Deity:** Moradin (The Soul Forger)

## Ability Scores
- **Strength:** 15 (+2)
- **Dexterity:** 10 (+0)
- **Constitution:** 16 (+3)
- **Intelligence:** 10 (+0)
- **Wisdom:** 16 (+3)
- **Charisma:** 8 (-1)

## Combat Statistics
- **Armor Class (AC):** 18 (Chain Mail + Shield)
- **Hit Points (HP):** 27
- **Speed:** 25 ft (not reduced by heavy armor)
- **Proficiency Bonus:** +2
- **Passive Perception:** 13

## Proficiencies & Languages
- **Armor:** Light armor, medium armor, heavy armor, shields
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons, battleaxe, handaxe, light hammer, warhammer
- **Tools:** Smith's tools, Mason's tools
- **Saving Throws:** Wisdom, Charisma
- **Skills:** Athletics, History, Insight, Religion
- **Languages:** Common, Dwarvish, Undercommon

## Features & Traits
- **Dwarven Resilience:** Advantage on saving throws against poison, resistance against poison damage.
- **Stonecunning:** Double proficiency bonus on History checks related to origin of stonework.
- **Blessing of the Forge:** Imbue a magic +1 bonus to a suit of armor or weapon at the end of a long rest.
- **Channel Divinity: Artisan's Blessing:** Conduct a 1-hour ritual to craft a nonmagical metal item up to 100 gp value.
- **Spellcasting:** Cantrips (*Mending*, *Sacred Flame*, *Guidance*); Prepared Spells (*Searing Smite*, *Identify*, *Heat Metal*, *Magic Weapon*, *Cure Wounds*, *Bless*).

## Equipment
- Warhammer, Shield with Moradin's anvil symbol
- Chain Mail, Light Hammer
- Smith's Tools, Guild Intro Letter
- Traveler's Clothes, Belt Pouch
- Priest's Pack, 20 gold pieces
