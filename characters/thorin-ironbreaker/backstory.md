# Backstory: Thorin Ironbreaker

Deep within the subterranean smithies of Citadel Adbar, Thorin spent a century honing his craft as a master blacksmith. To Thorin, hammering molten steel on an anvil was not merely labor—it was a holy prayer to Moradin, the All-Father who shaped dwarves from earth and flame.

Thorin's quiet artisan life changed when an ancient, corrupted relic threatened his clan's forge. Guiding the power of Moradin's holy forge, Thorin recast the corrupted weapon into a sacred divine symbol, purging its evil and saving his kin. Recognizing the sign from his deity, the high priests ordained Thorin as a cleric of the Forge Domain.

Leaving his forge behind, Thorin ventured into the surface world. He seeks rare minerals, ancient forging techniques, and companions worthy of wielding the masterwork armaments he creates in Moradin's honor.
