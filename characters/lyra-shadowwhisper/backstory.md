# Backstory: Lyra Shadowwhisper

Lyra grew up on the cobble-paved alleyways of the sprawling metropolis of Waterdeep. Orphaned at a young age due to prejudice against her fiendish heritage, she learned early that survival required quick fingers and quicker wits. 

Rather than succumbing to bitterness, Lyra used her talents to protect fellow street children from corrupt city watchmen and predatory thieves' guilds. A chance encounter with a reclusive wizard led to her acquiring a dusty tome of illusion magic, which she mastered on her own to enhance her natural agility with subtle arcana.

Now a rogue with a penchant for Robin Hood-style antics, Lyra lives by a simple code: steal from the tyrannical, protect the helpless, and never let anyone see you sweat. She travels in search of adventure, wealth, and ancient secrets that might help her uncover the true identity of her lost tiefling lineage.
