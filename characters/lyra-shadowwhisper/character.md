# Lyra Shadowwhisper

## Basic Information
- **Race:** Tiefling
- **Class:** Rogue (Arcane Trickster) - Level 3
- **Background:** Urchin
- **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Deity:** Olidammara (The Laughing Rogue)

## Ability Scores
- **Strength:** 8 (-1)
- **Dexterity:** 16 (+3)
- **Constitution:** 12 (+1)
- **Intelligence:** 14 (+2)
- **Wisdom:** 10 (+0)
- **Charisma:** 15 (+2)

## Combat Statistics
- **Armor Class (AC):** 14 (Leather Armor + Dex)
- **Hit Points (HP):** 21
- **Speed:** 30 ft
- **Proficiency Bonus:** +2
- **Passive Perception:** 14

## Proficiencies & Languages
- **Armor:** Light armor
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons, hand crossbows, longswords, rapiers, shortswords
- **Tools:** Thieves' tools, Disguise kit
- **Saving Throws:** Dexterity, Intelligence
- **Skills:** Acrobatics, Deception, Sleight of Hand (Expertise), Stealth (Expertise), Perception
- **Languages:** Common, Infernal, Thieves' Cant

## Features & Traits
- **Darkvision:** 60 ft range.
- **Hellish Resistance:** Resistance to fire damage.
- **Mage Hand Legerdemain:** Create an invisible Mage Hand to pick locks, pocket items, or retrieve objects.
- **Sneak Attack:** Deal +2d6 extra damage once per turn against a target you have advantage against or has an enemy nearby.
- **Cunning Action:** Bonus action to Dash, Disengage, or Hide.
- **Spellcasting:** Cantrips (*Mage Hand*, *Minor Illusion*, *Fire Bolt*); Level 1 Spells (*Disguise Self*, *Charm Person*, *Shield*).

## Equipment
- Rapier, Shortbow with quiver of 20 arrows
- Leather Armor, Daggers (2)
- Thieves' Tools, Disguise Kit
- Token of remembrance from her parents, Common Clothes
- Burglar's Pack, 15 gold pieces
