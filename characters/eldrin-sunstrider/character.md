# Eldrin Sunstrider

## Basic Information
- **Race:** High Elf
- **Class:** Paladin (Oath of Devotion) - Level 3
- **Background:** Noble
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Deity:** Lathander (The Morninglord)

## Ability Scores
- **Strength:** 16 (+3)
- **Dexterity:** 10 (+0)
- **Constitution:** 14 (+2)
- **Intelligence:** 10 (+0)
- **Wisdom:** 12 (+1)
- **Charisma:** 16 (+3)

## Combat Statistics
- **Armor Class (AC):** 18 (Chain Mail + Shield)
- **Hit Points (HP):** 28
- **Speed:** 30 ft
- **Proficiency Bonus:** +2
- **Passive Perception:** 13

## Proficiencies & Languages
- **Armor:** All armor, shields
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
- **Tools:** Dragonchess set
- **Saving Throws:** Wisdom, Charisma
- **Skills:** Athletics, History, Insight, Persuasion
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Celestial, Dwarvish

## Features & Traits
- **Fey Ancestry:** Advantage on saving throws against being charmed; magic cannot put you to sleep.
- **Trance:** Elves don't need to sleep; 4 hours of meditation provides the benefits of a long rest.
- **Divine Sense:** Detect celestial, fiend, or undead within 60 feet.
- **Lay on Hands:** Pool of 15 HP per long rest to heal wounds or cure diseases.
- **Fighting Style (Defense):** +1 bonus to AC while wearing armor.
- **Divine Smite:** Expend spell slots to deal radiant damage on melee weapon hits.
- **Sacred Weapon (Channel Divinity):** Add Charisma modifier (+3) to attack rolls for 1 minute; weapon emits bright light.

- Shield with emblem of Lathander
