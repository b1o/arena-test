# Backstory: Eldrin Sunstrider

Born into the prominent Sunstrider house of the high elven kingdom, Eldrin enjoyed a youth of privilege, philosophy, and martial tradition. However, the opulent halls of his estate could not shield him from the suffering beyond the enclave's borders. 

During a diplomatic mission to a border township, Eldrin witnessed a raid by fiendish cultists that devastated the local population. Standing alongside the defenders, he raised his blade and prayed for the strength to shield the innocent. In that moment, the warm amber glow of Lathander, the Morninglord, bathed his blade, turning back the dark tide.

Bound by a solemn oath of devotion, Eldrin renounced his comfortable noble inheritance to walk the open road as a paladin. He seeks to bring light to dark places, restore hope to the downtrodden, and uphold honor wherever corruption rears its head. Though burdened by the expectations of his family, his true loyalty lies with his sacred duty to defend mortal realms from shadow.
